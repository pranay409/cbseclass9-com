Surface Areas and Volumes for CBSE Class 9

Complete Formula Sheet, Tricks & Exam Patterns

Published on November 07, 2026

Surface Areas and Volumes is a critical chapter in CBSE Class 9 Mathematics that appears in the board exam and entrance tests. This chapter, as per NCERT Chapter 13, tests your understanding of three-dimensional geometry and requires strong visualization and formula application skills. Whether you're preparing for your unit tests or the final board exam, this comprehensive guide covers all essential formulas, tricks, and common mistakes you must avoid.

Understanding 3D Shapes: The Foundation

Before diving into formulas, it's essential to understand what surface area and volume mean. Surface Area is the total area covered by the surface of a 3D object, measured in square units. Volume is the amount of space occupied by a 3D object, measured in cubic units.

In CBSE Class 9, you'll primarily deal with six types of 3D shapes: cubes, cuboids, spheres, hemispheres, cylinders, and cones. Each has unique formulas for surface area and volume that you need to memorize and apply accurately.

The key to mastering this chapter is visualizing the shapes clearly. When you can mentally "unfold" a cylinder or "slice" a cone, applying formulas becomes intuitive. Use online 3D shape models or physical objects like dice, water bottles, and ice cream cones to develop this visualization skill.

NCERT Chapter 13: Essential Formulas for All Shapes

According to the NCERT curriculum, you must know the formulas for the following shapes. Let's organize them by shape type:

1. Cube and Cuboid

Cube - Surface Area SA = 6a²

where 'a' = side length

Cube - Volume V = a³

where 'a' = side length

Cuboid - Surface Area SA = 2(lw + wh + hl)

l=length, w=width, h=height

Cuboid - Volume V = l × w × h

l=length, w=width, h=height

2. Cylinder

Curved Surface Area CSA = 2πrh

r=radius, h=height

Total Surface Area TSA = 2πr(r + h)

r=radius, h=height

Volume V = πr²h

r=radius, h=height

3. Cone

Curved Surface Area CSA = πrl

r=radius, l=slant height

Total Surface Area TSA = πr(r + l)

r=radius, l=slant height

Volume V = (1/3)πr²h

r=radius, h=height

Slant Height Relation l² = r² + h²

Pythagorean theorem application

4. Sphere and Hemisphere

Sphere - Surface Area SA = 4πr²

r=radius

Sphere - Volume V = (4/3)πr³

r=radius

Hemisphere - Curved Surface Area CSA = 2πr²

r=radius
